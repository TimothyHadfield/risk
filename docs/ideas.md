# Data, exploration and theory ideas for Risk

| # | Idea | Call | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Exact win chance on every target, drawn on the map the moment you pick an attacker | **Build** (done) | Odds right where the decision is made; exact Markov chain, no simulation |
| 2 | Attack panel: win %, expected losses both sides, roll 1/2/3 dice or blitz | **Build** (done) | Shows the cost, not just the chance |
| 3 | Chain calculator: chance to take a path of territories, at least k of them, armies left at the end | **Build** (done, Odds tab) | The real question in Risk is "can I sweep this continent" |
| 4 | "Stack needed" for 50/75/90% against a defender | **Build** (done) | Turns odds into a placement decision |
| 5 | Win-chance curve by stack size | **Build** (done) | Shows the steep middle of the curve |
| 6 | Dice luck meter per player (kills minus expected kills) | **Build** (done) | Settles "the dice hate me" arguments with numbers |
| 7 | Continent value table: bonus per border to guard | **Build** (done, Study tab) | Classic Risk theory, why Australia/South America are prized |
| 8 | Threat lens: colour your land red to green by enemy armies next door | **Build** (done) | Where to put armies, at a glance |
| 9 | Hint button: best placement and best attack from the Hard brain | **Build** (done) | Study mode |
| 10 | Card ladder: set values and which set is next | **Build** (done) | The escalation drives the endgame |
| 11 | Who wins from here: play the game out 40 times with Hard in every seat | **Build** (done, Study tab) | A live "forecast" from the current position |
| 12 | Land-by-round timeline after the game | **Build** (done) | Post-game story of the war |
| 13 | Measured computer strength table behind a ? | **Build** (done) | Tim's "measured strength" rule |
| 14 | Real-table helper: tap a territory in the Odds tab to load its armies | **Build** (done) | Use at the physical table |
| 15 | Defender dice advisor (when to roll 1 die) | Skip | Rolling 2 is never worse for the defender here; nothing to advise |
| 16 | Trade-now-or-hold advisor | Skip | Needs a model of every opponent's hand; would be guesswork |
| 17 | "Which starting continent wins most" from 10,000 simulated games | Skip for now | Results would describe our bots, not people; revisit with online game logs |
| 18 | Heatmap of where wars happen (captures per territory) across a game | Skip | Fun but low decision value; timeline + luck cover the post-game story |
